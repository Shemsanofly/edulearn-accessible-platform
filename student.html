Interactive Algebra Notes

Algebra is a branch of mathematics dealing with symbols and the rules for manipulating those symbols.

Basic Concepts:

Variables

Symbols (usually letters) that represent unknown values or quantities that can change.

x, y, z
Constants

Fixed numerical values that don't change in an equation.

5, -3, π
Expressions

Mathematical phrases that combine variables and constants using operations.

2x + 5

Worked Examples

Example 1: Solving Linear Equations

Problem: Solve for x: 3x + 7 = 22

Step 1: Subtract 7 from both sides

3x + 7 - 7 = 22 - 7

3x = 15

Step 2: Divide both sides by 3

3x ÷ 3 = 15 ÷ 3

x = 5

Example 2: Expanding Expressions

Problem: Expand: 2(x + 3) + 4x

Step 1: Distribute the 2

2 × x + 2 × 3 + 4x = 2x + 6 + 4x

Step 2: Combine like terms

6x + 6

Quick Study Tips

1

Active Recall

Test yourself regularly instead of just rereading. This strengthens memory retention by 50%.

2

Spaced Repetition

Review material at increasing intervals: 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month.

3

Pomodoro Technique

Study for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break. After 4 cycles, take a longer 30-minute break.

4

Mind Mapping

Create visual diagrams to connect ideas. This helps with understanding complex topics and memory.

5

Teach Others

Explain concepts to friends or family. If you can teach it, you truly understand it.

6

Environment Setup

Create a dedicated, distraction-free study space with good lighting and comfortable temperature.
